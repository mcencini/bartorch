Dependencies#

The following are installed as separate packages, each under its own license, and are not redistributed by bartorch: PyTorch, NumPy, SciPy, FINUFFT and cuFINUFFT (Apache-2.0), TorchSim, MRI-NUFFT, and the optional MKL and DeepInverse. The CUDA wheel links the CUDA runtime, cuFFT and cuBLAS dynamically and does not contain them.
