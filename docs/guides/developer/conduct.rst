Code of conduct
===============

Community expectations
----------------------

Make collaboration welcoming to people with different backgrounds, experience
and identities. Be respectful, explain technical disagreement with evidence,
accept corrections, and credit others' contributions. These expectations apply
to issues, pull requests, reviews and project discussions.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, sexualized attention, personal
attacks and publication of another person's private information are unacceptable.
Focus criticism on work and behavior, and respect requests to stop.

Reporting and governance status
-------------------------------

This page proposes community expectations for the developing project. The
repository does not yet designate a confidential reporting contact or formal
enforcement process. Maintainers should establish those before presenting this
as an adopted, enforceable policy. Do not post sensitive incident details in a
public issue; use a maintainer's explicitly published private contact or the
hosting platform's reporting mechanism where applicable.
