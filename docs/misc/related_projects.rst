Related projects
================

* `BART <https://mrirecon.codeberg.page/>`_: the embedded reconstruction engine
  and source of command documentation and teaching resources.
* `PyTorch <https://pytorch.org/>`_: tensors, device selection, signal models
  and learning workflows.
* `DeepInverse <https://deepinv.org/>`_: inverse-problem physics, optimization
  and learning; see its `custom physics tutorial
  <https://deepinv.org/auto_examples/basics/demo_custom_physics.html>`_.
* `FINUFFT <https://finufft.readthedocs.io/>`_: nonuniform Fourier transforms,
  with cuFINUFFT providing the CUDA counterpart used by bartorch.
* `SigPy <https://sigpy.readthedocs.io/>`_: another Python MRI reconstruction
  library, useful as an independent implementation when checking conventions.
* `MRIReco.jl <https://github.com/MagneticResonanceImaging/MRIReco.jl>`_: MRI
  reconstruction in Julia.

Within the surrounding development workspace, ``torchsim`` and ``mrtoeplitz``
are relevant signal-model and normal-operator projects. Their presence here
is not a declared bartorch dependency or a guarantee of a supported adapter.
